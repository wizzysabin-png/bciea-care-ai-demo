# Breast awareness
Breast awareness means knowing what is normal for your own breasts and seeking medical advice when you notice a new or unusual change. A new breast lump, persistent skin change, nipple change, unusual discharge, or a new underarm lump should be assessed by a qualified health professional. Many breast changes are not cancer, but new or persistent changes should not be ignored.
